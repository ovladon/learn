LEARN / CAREER TOOLBOX · FREE FOREVER*

Make a QR code for your portfolio.

Generate a black-and-white QR image in your own browser. It points directly to your website; nobody has to subscribe to a QR service. *The generator is free; your QR remains useful while the destination URL is active.

Enter your portfolio address first. / Introdu întâi adresa portofoliului.

For a reliable QR / Pentru un cod sigur

  • Publish and open your portfolio before making the QR. / Publică și deschide site-ul înainte.
  • Use the complete https:// address, not a paid short link. / Folosește adresa completă.
  • Scan your printed QR with a different phone before sharing. / Scanează codul tipărit cu alt telefon.
  • Keep the same GitHub username and repository address. / Păstrează aceeași adresă.
  • Do not include passwords, tokens or private pages. / Nu include parole sau pagini private.

Privacy: This tool sends nothing to a QR provider. The code simply encodes the URL you enter.
