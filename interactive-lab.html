01 / DATA CENTERS

Who gets the next server?

Requests wait in a queue when all workers are busy. Try first-in, first-out (FIFO), then priority-based ordering.

    03 / CACHING

    Remember or recalculate?

    A small cache holds three results. Repeating a request can be cheaper, but an old cached answer can become stale.

    TRY IT YOURSELFRequest A twice. Then update A at the origin and request it again. Is the cheaper cached response still correct?

    Simplification: cache hit = 1 cost unit, origin fetch = 7 cost units. These are teaching numbers, not real pricing. LRU means 'least recently used'; correctness also needs invalidation or expiration.

    Who gets the hardware?

    Simplification: CPU/RAM/GPU tokens are invented capacities, not actual hardware quotas. Skipping a large job can improve short-term use but may cause starvation.
